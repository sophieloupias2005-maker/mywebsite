Service & Cultural Immersion

I had the privilege to go to Nepal with a group of volunteers following the devastating 2015 earthquake, as a member of our team had family living in the region.

During this trip, I got to immerse myself fully in Nepalese culture—discovering new foods, religious traditions, and deeply inspiring community values.

Our primary mission was to serve the community as best we could through distributing essential donations, helping rebuild local infrastructure, serving food, and bringing supplies and entertainment for children.

My fondest memory from the trip was volunteering at a Nepalese school. I had noticed that the boys and girls were separated by gender- the girls were doing arts and crafts with some volunteers while the boys were all playing soccer. I decided to join in on the soccer game and agreed to play goalie as no one else was willing to play that position. It ended up being a blast, just experiencing the pure joy of school recess soccer.
