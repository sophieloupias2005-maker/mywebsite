My Background

On this page you can find information about my studies, my life growing up in two countries, and what got me to where I am today.

Growing Up Multicultural

I grew up in San Diego, CA in a multicultural household—my mother is korean and my father is french. Therefore I am perfectly bilingual in French and English and speak elementary level Korean.

In 2019, my family decided to move to France where I have lived since. I consider myself very lucky to have been exposed to many different cultures as I have developed a love of travel, discovering and appreciating new cultures, and meeting new people.

My Studies in the US

I attended Pacific Trails Middle School in San Diego, CA where my love for science began: I joined the Science Olympiad team to learn about astronomy and herpetology. I also involved myself fully in extra-curriculars that the school had to offer—I participated in band and joined the volleyball team.

I completed my freshman year of high school at Canyon Crest Academy in San Diego where I pursued band and decided to try various arts such as theater and intro to art.

My Studies in France

I am currently a student at ENSEEIHT, a French engineering school based in Toulouse.

After completing a two-year intensive program (Classe Préparatoire aux Grandes Écoles) in Toulouse, I passed the nationwide entrance exams for engineering schools. This program is very specific to the French higher education system and focuses heavily on high-level mathematics and physics.

I am now pursuing a Master's degree specializing in the field of electrical energy and electronics.
