Activities & Passions

This page is dedicated to my creative, physical, and personal pursuits—explore what drives me outside of my engineering studies.

Arts

I found a real passion for fine art in high school, where I started experimenting with acrylics and pencil drawing. Over the years, I have broadened my horizons to oil paint, charcoal, and ballpoint pen.

I am also a passionate art and art history enthusiast, and over the years I have had the opportunity to connect with inspiring art collectors and gallerists who I now consider friends.

Here is a link to my art portfolio where you can find a collection of personal artworks I have created and am particularly proud of. I hope you enjoy exploring my artistic journey.

Sports

I've practiced many different sports throughout my life in an effort to maintain a healthy and balanced lifestyle.

Most notably, I did competitive gymnastics for 6 years as part of the La Jolla YMCA team. We attended regional and national competitions throughout the U.S. as well as the World Gymnaestrada in Finland. These years taught me teamwork, diligence in training, consistency, determination, and autonomy at a very young age.

I played volleyball for 2 years in middle school, where I was a part of the girls' team and we played against other middle schools in the district. This was a new experience for me as I had never played volleyball competitively before. I learned to take risks and challenge myself in a new sport.

Currently, I practice various sports and physical activities for fun. I take part in yoga classes with a group of americans living in France and I also regularly attend reformer pilate classes. I am picking up tennis and badminton and regularly play with friends. I also tried rock climbing which I greatly enjoyed and met some interesting people in the climbing community. All in all, I'm using my free time to try out as many different activities as possible and meeting amazing new people in the process.

Literature

I have always been an avid reader, since I was a child. Reading is extremely important to me, especially growing up bilingual as it has been a way for me to keep practicing the language of the country I am not currently living in. While I was in the US, I forced myself to read in French to keep up my vocabulary and grammar skills. Now that I am living in France, I have been enjoying english and american classics. I also believe that reading is a social activity, and a good way to meet people and discuss different perspectives. Therefore, alongside some friends, I started a book club at my school, ENSEEIHT, where we share our favorite books and discuss them.
