Sustainability & Civic Engagement

I am very involved in serving and giving back to my community. My priority at the moment is fighting overconsumption and fast fashion which exploitative and harmful to the environment.

Second-Hand Clothing

In an effort to reduce my carbon footprint, I set a goal for myself: to not buy new. Therefore, for the past 4 years, I have exclusively shopped at local thrift stores (Emmaüs in France, Goodwill, The Salvation Army, and local benefit shops in the U.S.) or on online second-hand platforms like Vinted, Depop, and eBay. More than 90% of my closet is second hand as well as all my books, electronics, furniture and home appliances. I also actively donate or sell my used clothing to give a second life.

Arts & Sports

In parallel to my engineering studies, I have been deeply invested in physical activity as well as various art forms.

Art

I found a real passion for art in high school, where I started experimenting with acrylics and pencil drawing. Over the years, I have broadened my horizons to oil paint, charcoal, and ballpoint pen. I am also a huge art and art history fan and have met some amazing art collectors and gallerists who I now consider friends!

Sports & Gymnastics

I've practiced many different sports throughout my life in an effort to lead a healthy lifestyle.

Most notably, I did competitive gymnastics for 6 years as part of the La Jolla YMCA team. We attended competitions throughout the U.S. as well as the World Gymnaestrada in Finland. These years taught me teamwork, diligence in training, consistency, determination, and autonomy at a very young age.
