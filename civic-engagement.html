This page is dedicated to my civic engagements, volunteer initiatives, and community service work for causes I care deeply about.

Volunteer Work & Community Service

Alongside my engineering studies, I give back to my community by applying both operational support and technical problem-solving to local non-profit initiatives.

Les Restos du Cœur (Albi Branch)

I actively volunteer with Les Restos du Cœur in my hometown of Albi, collaborating directly with the branch president, Brigitte Debras. I was introduced to the leadership through a friend who directs the branch's agricultural reintegration initiative (Jardin d'insertion). I contribute to the organization through a mix of hands-on operational support and digital process optimization, applying both my technical problem-solving and interpersonal skills across a range of tasks.

Digital & Process Optimization: Leveraging my technical background in Excel and programming, I streamline administrative workflows to enhance efficiency. Most recently, I developed an automated spreadsheet system to process beneficiary files and calculate individual family food allowances based on eligibility criteria.

Food Distribution: I assist on the front lines during food distribution, guiding beneficiaries through the pantry setting to help them select balanced, nutritious options tailored to their dietary restrictions.

Cross-Cultural Communication: As a fluent English speaker, I bridge communication gaps for non-French-speaking individuals, assisting other volunteers and ensuring that non-native visitors receive clear, welcoming support.

Fundraising: I participate in fundraising campaigns, notably organizing holiday gift-wrapping stations to generate donations and raise public awareness.

Sustainability & Ethical Living

I am deeply committed to sustainable resource management. My priority is fighting overconsumption and fast fashion, which are both exploitative and environmentally damaging.

Second-Hand & Circular Economy Commitment

In an effort to reduce my carbon footprint, I set a personal goal: to avoid buying new manufactured goods whenever possible. For the past four years, I have exclusively shopped at local thrift stores (Emmaüs in France, Goodwill, The Salvation Army, and local benefit shops in the U.S.) and online second-hand platforms like Vinted, Depop, and eBay.

More than 90% of my wardrobe, as well as my books, electronics, furniture, and home appliances, are second-hand. I also actively donate or resell my used items to keep them in circulation and promote a circular economy.
